# SAME-DAY RELEASE — Grok Imagine Mega Prompt Pack
### A long cinematic movie of Boss Jed, MCARS PH, the hauler, and the last-quarter lot

Grok Imagine does **not** spit out a 90-minute film from one box. Each generation is **6 / 10 / 15 seconds**. A long movie is made by locking the character, generating shot 01, then hitting **Extend Video** with the next beat, over and over. This pack is that film.

**Settings every time**
- Aspect: **16:9** (cinematic) — switch to **9:16** only if you want a Reels cut
- Duration: **15 seconds** per clip, then Extend
- Quality: highest / 1080p if offered
- Attach these as **image references** on every shot: a photo of Jed (the glasses + grin), the MCARS PH red logo, a red jacket still if you have one
- In the prompt, tag them: `@Jed` `@Logo` `@Jacket`

**How to shoot the long movie**
1. Generate the **identity stills** first (prompts at the bottom). Keep those images pinned as references.
2. Paste **SHOT 01** → generate 15s.
3. Click **Extend** → paste **SHOT 02** (do **not** re-describe the whole world — only what happens next).
4. Repeat through **SHOT 48**. That is ~12 minutes of movie. Loop Acts 3–4 with the extra beats if you want it longer.
5. If a clip drifts (wrong glasses, wrong jacket), regenerate that shot from the still, then continue Extending from the good one.

## 1. THE MOVIE — what you are actually making

**Title:** SAME-DAY RELEASE  
**Logline:** A Malabon closer who started with bikes and a ₱100,000 dream builds MCARS PH into a nationwide machine — one hauler, 17,000 keys, and a last-quarter quota that will not close itself.

**True research baked in (do not “Hollywood-ify” these facts):**
- Real name John Michael “Jed” Manalang. Founder & CEO of MCARS PH.
- Started as a hustler: online game items, English bulldogs, cellphones sold to classmates’ mothers and teachers, then a motor shop at the family house.
- 2020 pandemic: high-end bikes under MBikes, supplying Luzon / Visayas / Mindanao. Realized selling a bike took the same grind as selling a car, with a bigger life attached.
- 2020–2021 pivot: brand-new, second-hand, assume-balance units. MCARS PH launches. Main lot at **95 Yanga St., Brgy. Maysilo, Malabon**.
- The product is not “luxury flex.” It is **pang-masa ownership**: rent-to-own, assume balance / pasalo, trade-in, in-house financing, **one-day process, same-day release, no bank approval**. Two valid IDs, proof of billing, proof of income. COD nationwide.
- Clients come from Baler, Bicol, Cavite, Tarlac, OFWs flying in from Australia and going straight to the lot. Live selling on Facebook for hours. Open till 8pm, he’ll wait till midnight.
- 16,000–17,000+ successful transactions. Elite Agent Platform (2025, Music Box, Timog) — ordinary Filipinos earn ₱5k–₱100k commission per unit, no capital.
- Units on the board in this film: 2018 Vios (starter), Wigo, Honda City, 2016 Fortuner, Civic, Pajero “presidential presence, sedan price,” Camaro ZL1 traded for a Dodge Challenger, Land Cruiser, Alphard (Boss Jed’s pick), Xpander, CR-V, Lexus RX (last unit).
- Awards energy (tasteful, not a trophy commercial): Best CEO, Best Car Dealership Firm, Dealer of the Year — shown as a blink, not the point.
- The game’s map is the second half of the movie: Malabon Showroom → EDSA Gauntlet → C5 Ortigas → BGC High Line → NLEX Nationwide (the hauler’s highway) → Timog Elite Agent / Music Box → Cubao, Makati, Clark, Cebu, Davao, Alabang. Arcade fever-dreams: EDSA Rush driving, Survival on the lot, Quota Invaders, Jed’s Deal (banker calling), Replevin Cells.

**Theme:** Trust is a same-day act. Keys turn into cars. Buy is the minus. Sell is the plus. Quota does not close itself.

# PART E — RULES THAT KEEP THE LONG MOVIE FROM FALLING APART

1. **Extend = next action only.** If you re-paste the whole bible into an Extend, Imagine will reset the scene and you’ll lose continuity.
2. **If the glasses disappear, stop.** Regenerate that shot from Still 1. The glasses are the face.
3. **If the hauler becomes an American 18-wheeler, stop.** Re-attach Still 3.
4. **One location change per extend** is safer than three.
5. **Dialogue is short.** Imagine mangles long speeches. One line per shot.
6. **Do not name licensed minigame shows or other car brands in a logo-close-up** if a shot gets blocked — say “black briefcases with red seals” and “white compact sedan / pearl SUV / black luxury van.”
7. **Keep Jed kind.** The real closer’s brand is *kami na ang bahala*, not wolf-of-Wall-Street.
8. To push past 12 minutes: after Shot 40, do not stop — run 41–48, then re-Enter Act III from a new angle (“another night on the lot”) and extend again. The quota is a loop. That is the point of the game and the man.
